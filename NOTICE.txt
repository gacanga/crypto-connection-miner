Crypto Connection Miner 0.6.0 (named GostMiner up to 0.4.0) - multi-coin GPU miner for NVIDIA (gostd: GOSTcoin,
lyra2v2: Bitmark, neoscrypt: Feathercoin), no dev fee.

Why GPL-3: the lyra2v2 and neoscrypt GPU kernels come from ccminer 2.3.1 (tpruvot, GPL-3), as improved in MYMINER.
